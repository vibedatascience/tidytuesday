# Recorded ship losses around Ireland by decade

![Recorded ship losses around Ireland by decade](20260630.png)

[Python code](20260630.py) · [Source dataset](https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/tree/main/data/2026/2026-06-30)

Validate unique wreck IDs, retain known loss years 1800–2024 and bin by decade. No missing year is imputed. The 2020s are partial. Highlight the 1910s and 1940s without attributing every loss in those decades to war.

Data: National Monuments Service Wreck Inventory of Ireland. Chart: vibedatascience.

Inputs (original CSV files, losslessly gzip-compressed): [wreck_inventory.csv.gz](data/wreck_inventory.csv.gz)
